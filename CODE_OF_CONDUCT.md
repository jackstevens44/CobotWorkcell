# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free community for contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and evidence.
- Welcome questions and different levels of robotics experience.
- Protect personal data and avoid encouraging unsafe physical testing.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect safety, security, and project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate publication of secrets, and instructions intended to bypass physical safety controls are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
