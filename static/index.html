Place and measure the reference tags

Where is X=0, Y=0?
The XY origin is the center of the robot's first rotating joint (J1), projected straight down onto the work surface. It is not the front edge of the base. +X points straight forward from the robot and +Y points to the robot's left.

Put tags 0-3 flat near the workspace corners. Measure from that projected J1 center to the center of each black tag. Enter meters; right of the robot is negative Y.

Use one unassigned object tag (ID 10–25) as the calibration target. Move it to nine measured positions across the usable table, enter its expected X/Y, then click Read Camera. Practical mode allows 10 mm RMS, 20 mm maximum error, and 5 mm stationary spread. This never moves the robot.
